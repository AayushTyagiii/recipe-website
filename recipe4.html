Veg Burger

Preparation Time: 20 minutes

Difficulty Level: Easy

Description

Veg Burger is a popular fast food made with a crispy vegetable patty, fresh vegetables, and soft burger buns. It is quick to prepare and very tasty.

This dish is perfect for snacks or light meals and can be made easily at home.

Ingredients

  • Burger buns
  • Vegetable patty
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Tomato slices
  • Onion slices
  • Mayonnaise or ketchup

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Lightly toast the burger buns.
  2. Cook the vegetable patty until crispy.
  3. Spread mayonnaise or ketchup on buns.
  4. Place lettuce, patty, tomato, and onion.
  5. Cover with the top bun.
  6. Serve hot.

Tips

Add cheese slice for extra taste. Toast buns with butter for better flavor.
