White Sauce Pasta

Preparation Time: 25 minutes

Difficulty Level: Easy

Description

White Sauce Pasta is a creamy and delicious Italian dish made using milk, butter, and cheese. It is one of the easiest pasta recipes and is loved for its smooth texture and rich taste.

This dish is perfect for quick meals and can be customized with different vegetables and spices. It is especially popular among students because it is simple to prepare and very satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup pasta
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • Vegetables (capsicum, corn, carrot)
  • Salt and black pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until soft, then drain and keep aside.
  2. Heat butter in a pan and add flour. Cook for 1 minute.
  3. Slowly add milk while stirring continuously to avoid lumps.
  4. Add cheese and mix until the sauce becomes smooth.
  5. Add vegetables and cook for a few minutes.
  6. Add boiled pasta and mix everything well.
  7. Cook for 2–3 minutes and serve hot.

Tips

Always stir the sauce continuously while adding milk to avoid lumps. You can add oregano or chili flakes for extra flavor.
